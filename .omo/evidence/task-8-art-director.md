# Task 8 Art Director Review

Overall: approved.

## 1. Visual hierarchy and typography

- Strong hierarchy: the title is immediately dominant, then score, then CTA.
- The neon type treatment fits the cabinet aesthetic well.
- Minor issue: the smallest helper text ("ARROW KEYS / WASD - MOVE" and the gameover secondary lines) gets a little too faint and could be one step brighter for readability.

## 2. Color harmony and contrast

- Excellent palette discipline: electric blue frame, warm yellow title, white UI text, and accent colors feel cohesive.
- Contrast is strong against the black field and reads like a real arcade attract screen.
- The red gameover state and cyan resume/CTA accents work without breaking the palette.

## 3. Transition smoothness and visual artifacts

- Transitions read smooth in both the 125ms and 350ms frames.
- No obvious tearing or layout jumps; the motion feels like a clean cabinet UI swap.
- Minor polish note: the glow/blur can make the text feel slightly hazy in mid-transition, so keep an eye on sharpness at the smallest sizes.

## 4. Overall aesthetic cohesion with the Neon Cabinet theme

- Very cohesive. It feels like a focused retro-futurist arcade cabinet, not just a neon skin.
- The screen framing, corner lights, and soft bloom sell the theme convincingly.
- The title, gameplay, gameover, and pause states all feel like part of one visual system.

## Verdict

Approved.
